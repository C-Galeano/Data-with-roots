Unsupervised Learning

K-Means by Hand: Manual Exercise

Three iterations of K-Means on 100 seed trays, calculated step by step without Scikit-learn so every distance and centroid can be traced.

01 · Dataset

Context of the Data

A plant nursery sows {{ dataset_stats.records }} seed trays under different irrigation routines. Fourteen days after sowing, two measurements are taken from each tray.

Moisture is one of the main drivers of germination. Seeds need water to start growing, but too much water pushes out oxygen and makes them rot, so the relationship is not linear. The nursery wants to know which irrigation profiles exist among its trays. There is no label to predict, which makes this a clustering problem.

Both variables are percentages on the same 0–100 scale, so the Euclidean distance can be calculated directly without normalizing the data.

Variables
Variable Meaning
Soil_Moisture_Percent (x) Average volumetric moisture of the tray's substrate. Low values mean dry soil, high values mean waterlogged soil.

03 · Iterations

Three Iterations Step by Step

In each iteration, the distance from every tray to the three current centroids is calculated, each tray is assigned to its nearest centroid (the smallest distance is highlighted), and the centroids are moved to the average of their trays.

Worked example: tray T001 = (39.4, 83.2) in iteration 1

d(T001, C1) = √((39.4 − 30)2 + (83.2 − 60)2) = √(88.36 + 538.24) = 25.03

d(T001, C2) = 41.26 and d(T001, C3) = 40.73, so T001 is assigned to C1.

04 · Variance

Within-Cluster Variance Across Iterations

The variance of a cluster is the mean squared distance from its trays to its updated centroid. The total WCSS (within-cluster sum of squares) adds the squared distances of all 100 trays. Lower values mean more compact clusters.

Conclusion

K-Means found three irrigation profiles that match agronomic intuition: germination peaks at intermediate moisture and falls on both sides. One variable alone could not show this. Moisture separates the under-watered trays from the over-watered ones, but only the combination with germination rate shows that the middle group performs best. Starting from poorly placed centroids, the algorithm corrected itself in two iterations and converged in the third.
