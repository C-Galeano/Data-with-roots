USE CASE 3

Customer Segmentation

Using Unsupervised Learning to discover groups of similar customers

Problem Context

Customers can have very different purchasing behaviors, preferences, and levels of activity. Treating every customer in the same way can make it difficult for businesses to create effective marketing strategies.

In many situations, businesses do not have predefined categories that accurately describe their customers.

Machine Learning can analyze customer data and discover groups of customers that have similar behaviors and characteristics.

Objective

The objective is to identify groups of customers with similar purchasing behaviors and characteristics.

Main Question

Which customers have similar behaviors and characteristics?

Data That Could Be Used

Purchase Frequency

How often a customer makes purchases.

Total Spending

Total amount of money spent by a customer.

Average Purchase Value

Average amount spent in each purchase.

Products Purchased

Types and quantities of products purchased by customers.

Customer Activity

Customer interactions with the business or platform.

Purchase History

Historical information about previous purchases.

How Does Machine Learning Work in This Case?

01
Data Collection

Customer information such as purchases, spending, and activity is collected.

02
Pattern Analysis

The algorithm analyzes similarities and differences between customers without predefined categories.

03
Customer Groups

The algorithm creates groups of customers that have similar behaviors and characteristics.

MACHINE LEARNING TYPE

Unsupervised Learning

Unsupervised Learning works with data that does not have predefined labels or categories. The algorithm discovers patterns and relationships within the data.

Key Idea: The model discovers groups without being given the correct group for each customer.
PROBLEM TYPE

Clustering

Clustering groups data points according to their similarities. In this case, customers are grouped according to their behaviors and characteristics.

Possible Output: Groups of Similar Customers

Potential Benefits

Understand Customers

Discover different customer behaviors and purchasing patterns.

Personalized Marketing

Create marketing strategies adapted to different customer groups.

Identify Valuable Groups

Identify customer groups with high levels of activity or spending.

Discover Hidden Patterns

Find relationships and customer groups that may not be obvious through manual analysis.

Case Summary

Customer Segmentation uses Unsupervised Learning to analyze customer behavior without predefined categories. The model solves a Clustering problem by discovering groups of customers with similar characteristics and purchasing behaviors.
