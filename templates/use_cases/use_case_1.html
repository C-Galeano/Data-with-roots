USE CASE 1

House Price Prediction

Using Supervised Learning to estimate the price of a property

Problem Context

The price of a house can depend on several characteristics, such as its size, location, number of bedrooms, number of bathrooms, and age of the property.

Estimating the value of a property manually can be difficult because many different factors can influence its price.

Machine Learning can analyze historical housing data and learn the relationship between the characteristics of a property and its actual selling price.

Objective

The objective is to estimate the approximate selling price of a house based on its characteristics and historical housing data.

Main Question

What could be the approximate price of this house?

Data That Could Be Used

House Area

The size of the property measured in square meters.

Bedrooms

Number of bedrooms available in the property.

Bathrooms

Number of bathrooms in the property.

Location

Geographic location or area where the property is located.

Property Age

Number of years since the property was built.

Historical Price

Previous selling prices of similar properties.

How Does Machine Learning Work in This Case?

01
Data Collection

Historical information about houses and their selling prices is collected.

02
Model Training

The model learns the relationship between house characteristics and their known selling prices.

03
Price Prediction

The trained model uses the characteristics of a new house to estimate its potential price.

MACHINE LEARNING TYPE

Supervised Learning

Supervised Learning uses training data that contains known answers. In this case, the model receives information about houses together with their actual selling prices.

Key Idea: The model learns from historical examples where the correct result is already known.
PROBLEM TYPE

Regression

Regression is used when the model needs to predict a numerical value. Here, the output is the estimated price of a property.

Possible Output: Estimated House Price

Potential Benefits

Estimate Property Values

Provide an estimated value for properties based on their characteristics.

Analyze Historical Data

Use previous property sales to identify useful relationships between different variables.

Identify Relationships

Discover how factors such as area, location, and property characteristics can affect price.

Support Decisions

Help buyers, sellers, and real estate businesses make more informed decisions.

Case Summary

House Price Prediction uses Supervised Learning to analyze historical housing data and learn the relationship between property characteristics and selling prices. The model solves a Regression problem by producing a numerical estimate of a property's potential price.
