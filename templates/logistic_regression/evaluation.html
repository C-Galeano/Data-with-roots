Interpretation

Understanding the Results

Confusion Matrix

True Positives (TP) are seeds that germinated and were correctly classified as germinated. True Negatives (TN) are seeds that did not germinate and were correctly classified.

False Positives (FP) are seeds predicted as germinated when they did not germinate. False Negatives (FN) are seeds that germinated but were classified as not germinated.

Model Performance

Accuracy measures the overall correctness of the model. Precision measures the reliability of positive predictions, while Recall measures how many actual germinated seeds were correctly identified.

The F1-Score combines Precision and Recall into a single balanced metric.
