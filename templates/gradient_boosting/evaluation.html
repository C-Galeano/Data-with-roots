Interpretation

Understanding the Results

Confusion Matrix

True Positives (TP) are fields that were actually at high risk of disease and were correctly classified as high risk. True Negatives (TN) are fields that were actually low risk and were correctly classified as low risk.

False Positives (FP) are low-risk fields incorrectly predicted as high risk. False Negatives (FN) are high-risk fields that were missed and classified as low risk, which is the costlier error in a crop disease scenario.

Model Performance

Accuracy measures the overall correctness of the model. Precision measures how reliable a "High Risk" prediction is, while Recall measures how many of the actual high-risk fields the model managed to catch.

The F1-Score combines Precision and Recall into a single balanced metric, which is useful when both false alarms and missed outbreaks carry a cost.
